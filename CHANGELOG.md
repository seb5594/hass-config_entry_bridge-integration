# Changelog

## 2026.11.26b1 (pre-release)

- ✨ First version. A dashboard card that shows a config entry like the settings page (state, gear, ⋮ menu, devices and entities) to people who are not administrators.
- Rights per person, saved and enforced on the server: what they see (settings, devices) and what they may do (open the gear, reload).
- Hide entities by checkbox or regular expression, for everybody or for one person; hidden entities never leave the server.
- Preview as any person; a card nobody may see does not exist for them.
- The integration serves and registers the card itself; English, German, French, Spanish, Italian and Dutch.
- Real Home Assistant tests on 2024.11, 2025.1, 2025.12, 2026.2 and 2026.9.
